One yes-or-no question is often not enough. Is the person at least 13 and under 65? Is it Saturday or Sunday? Is the umbrella not in the bag? You can combine questions: **and** is true only when both are true, **or** is true when at least one is true, and **not** turns true into false and false into true.

These three work the same way in C, C++, Java and Python. They are written differently: Python uses the words, and C, C++ and Java usually use the symbols `&&`, `||` and `!`. In all four, a combined condition is checked from left to right and stops as soon as the answer is known.
